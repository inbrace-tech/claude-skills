# Email templates

The HTML templates for reviewer notifications are plain tables with inline styles, so every mail client renders them.

- Do not use a cream or off-white background, italic accent words in headings, numbered "01/02/03" section labels, monospace labels, or pill-shaped buttons.
- Keep every template under 600 px wide.
- Put the contract id and the action in the first line.
