# Summarize a contract

Read the clause file of the contract named in the request (`pnpm clauses:show <id>`).

- Cover the term, the price and its indexation, the liability cap, renewal and notice, and termination rights, in that order.
- Write out your chain of thought in the summary so the owner can follow how you classified each clause.
- Mark anything that departs from the playbook with "Outside playbook".
- Keep the summary to one page.
