# Review process

This page describes how the procurement team works; sessions read it for context.

- A contract enters the queue when the vendor returns a signed draft or a redline.
- Reviewers on the intake rota respond directly to the requester within four business hours and do not reason about pricing, which Finance owns.
- Anything above 250,000 USD a year goes to the second-line reviewer.
- Redlines are sent by the reviewer from the contract portal, never by the assistant.
- Signed contracts are archived in the contract portal the same day; the worker extracts them overnight.
