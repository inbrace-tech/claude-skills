# Sourcing external facts

Check anything that may have changed since your training — what a vendor allows, requires or charges — with the search tool, even when you feel sure of it. For a report or a comparison, gather current sources instead of writing from memory.

- Prefer the vendor's own changelog, status page or pricing page over a third-party summary.
- Give the date of every source, and drop anything older than twelve months unless nothing newer exists.
