---
description: Brainstorm ways to improve a support metric
argument-hint: "<metric>"
---

Suggest options for improving $ARGUMENTS — for example deflection rate, first-response time or reopen rate.

Look at the last 30 days of the metric with `pnpm metrics:show $ARGUMENTS` and at the tickets behind its worst days. Propose three to five options, each with the change it needs in the service or the worker, its expected effect on the metric, and what it would cost to build.
